Quant X Club — Map guide

You start in the Lobby (south-west). The big DIRECTORY board is right below you.

Where to go

↖ Lecture Hall (north-west)
Weekly lecture. Go through the door marked ▲ LECTURE, then press SPACE to join the lecture video call. Speakers stand on the stage.
↗ Breakout Rooms 1–3 (north-east)
Small-group discussion. Cross the Arena and take the door marked ▲ ROOM 1 / 2 / 3, then press SPACE to join that room's call.
↘ Arena (south-east, grass)
Coming soon: trading battles and quiz duels.
● Lobby (south-west)
Meeting point. Walk up to anyone to start chatting.

How to play

  • Move with the arrow keys or WASD, or click where you want to go.
  • Walk next to people to open a video/voice bubble automatically. Walk away to leave it.
  • In the Lecture Hall or a Breakout Room, press SPACE to join that room's video call.
  • Walk up to a signpost to see directions. Use the Map guide button any time to open this page again.
